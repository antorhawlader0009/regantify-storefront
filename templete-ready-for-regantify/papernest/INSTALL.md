# Papernest: install into Regantify (not installed yet, kit only)

Template: `templete/others-templetes/Stationery & Education/papernest/papernest` (PAPERNEST, stationery, "Write · Create · Belong").
Design (colours exact, from the template's `tailwind.config.ts`): a white page, violet `#5B2E9E` (hover `#4A2487`, light `#7B4DC0`) for buttons, links and the top bar, navy `#1B2A4A` for headings and the footer, sun yellow `#FFD54F` and coral `#FF5C7A` for small highlights and tags, a pastel sky-pink-rose hero gradient, white rounded-xl cards with a faint violet-blue shadow, **Poppins** everywhere (exists, `--font-poppins`). A violet top bar (the Site Banner), a white header (the multi-colour leaf mark, a bold navy "PaperNest", a rounded search bar with a violet square button, Account, Wishlist and Cart with a coral count) and a thin-lined menu row under it, a pastel gradient hero (a small violet caption, a navy title with a pink-orange-teal last line, a violet button, the photo fading in from the right), a plain row of pastel circles for categories with two-line navy labels, a mint and a pink promo tile (a sun-yellow and a teal button), 6-across white cards with a violet cart button, a navy footer.
Built 2026-11-15 on the Greenmart kit's code (renamed `Papernest*`, prefix `pn-`, tokens `--pn-*`) because it already has a full-width pale hero with the photo fading in from the right, a white header over a bordered menu row and a dark footer; recoloured and restyled. Shared-file branches are copied from the `chicwove` ones like the other kits.

## Install
1. `node templete-ready-for-regantify/papernest/wire.js` from the workspace root (theme, demo route, CSS block, enum + migration, `papernest` branch next to every `chicwove` one in 29 shared files, THEME_ORDER entry last, Templates category **Stationery & Education**, `Trending Products` as the top-selling title). No new font. Rerun-safe.
2. Server: `prisma generate` (stop the API first), `migrate deploy` locally and on production through the tunnel, `tsc --noEmit`.
3. `tsc --noEmit` in storefront, `tsc -b` in client.
4. Browser check on `shopnet` only: home, list, product, cart, wishlist, checkout, account, Bangla switch, `/template-demo/papernest`, phone width, footer.
5. CLAUDE.md line, commit as Antor, push only when the owner says.

## What was verified (2026-11-15, repos untouched)
`wire.js` ran on a scratch copy of the storefront/server/client files (junction to node_modules); `tsc --noEmit` there was clean on the first run. Client and server were not type-checked (theme-name lists only), nothing was looked at in a browser. Repos stayed at 0 changes.

## Decisions and notes
- Plan: last in `THEME_ORDER`. Category: Stationery & Education.
- Home order: HERO, CATEGORY_SHORTCUTS (pastel circles), CAMPAIGNS (the mint and pink tiles from a store's own campaigns, up to two), TOP_SELLING, FLASH_SALE, CATEGORY_SECTIONS, HIGHLIGHTS, REVIEWS, MAGAZINE, FAQ, SOCIAL_FEED.
- **The template's category circles hold emoji; here they hold the store's own category photos** (the first letter on a pastel circle when a store has no picture). No emoji anywhere.
- Header: the template's search bar is the shared search panel's trigger (it opens the shared search on a click, it is not a text box). The violet top bar is StorePal's Site Banner (no banner text, no bar), so the template's currency picker and "Track Order" links are not drawn. The "Deals" menu link in rose and the active underline come from the store's own menu, not the template's fixed list.
- Left out (no feature, or made-up text): the floating paper plane, the handwritten note card on the hero (needs the Caveat font), the three hero icons (Study Better, Create More, Live Brighter), the newsletter band, the brand logo strip and the "inspiration" section (a store's own highlights, reviews and magazine cover them), the sample testimonials (a store's own reviews show), the currency picker.
- The footer is navy and re-points colour variables, so the call bar (generated by `wire.js`) uses fixed colours: a violet band with white buttons.
