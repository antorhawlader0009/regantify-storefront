Design (colours exact, from the template's `tailwind.config.ts`): a cream page `#FDF8EE`, a black header, hero and footer (`#111`, contact bar `#222`), gold `#F2C468` (deeper `#C9973A`, light `#F8DC9B`) for filled buttons **with dark text** and, in its deeper tone, for tags and links, ink `#141414`, white cards with a deep soft shadow and rounded-2xl corners, **Poppins** everywhere (exists). A black header (a gold-framed logo tile, white menu with a gold underline on the active link, round black gold-icon buttons for search, wishlist and the account in gold), a full-screen hero photo under black fades (a gold-outlined uppercase eyebrow pill, a huge white title with a gold second line, a gold pill button and a white outline pill), four image category cards with a white info panel overlapping the photo, a dark block with glass cards and gold-ringed icons, 3-across listing cards (4:3 photo, name, location line, bold price, a gold outline "View" button that fills gold on hover), a dark rounded contact bar with a gold button, a black footer with a gold call band.
Built 2026-11-15 on the Novatrend kit's code (renamed `Estatepro*`, prefix `ep-`, tokens `--ep-*`) because it already has a dark full-bleed hero and a black footer; its white header was made black. Shared-file branches are copied from the `chicwove` ones like the other kits.

## Install
1. `node templete-ready-for-regantify/estatepro/wire.js` from the workspace root (theme, demo route, CSS block, enum + migration, `estatepro` branch next to every `chicwove` one in 29 shared files, THEME_ORDER entry last). No new font. No `THEME_CATEGORIES` line: Templates has no "Properties" tab, so it shows under Others (add the tab to `CATEGORIES` in `Themes.tsx` first if the owner wants one). Rerun-safe.
2. Server: `prisma generate` (stop the API first), `migrate deploy` locally and on production through the tunnel, `tsc --noEmit`.
3. `tsc --noEmit` in storefront, `tsc -b` in client.
4. Browser check on `shopnet` only: home, list, product, cart, wishlist, checkout, account, Bangla switch, `/template-demo/estatepro`, phone width, footer.
5. CLAUDE.md line, commit as Antor, push only when the owner says.

## Decisions and notes
- Plan: last in `THEME_ORDER`. Category: none (Others).
- **Gold needs dark text.** Every filled gold button in the kit uses `text-ink`; the shared "accent fill, white text" buttons (checkout, cart and so on) get dark text from one CSS rule (`.bg-accent.text-white`). `--color-accent` is the deeper gold `#C9973A`, so gold text on cream or white stays readable.
- Home order: HERO, CATEGORY_SHORTCUTS (four image cards), HIGHLIGHTS (the dark block), TOP_SELLING (listing cards), CAMPAIGNS (the dark contact bar from a store's own campaign), FLASH_SALE, CATEGORY_SECTIONS, REVIEWS, MAGAZINE, FAQ, SOCIAL_FEED.
- The template is a property catalogue (beds, baths, area, For Sale / For Rent, a location search, an inquiry form). StorePal has none of those fields: a listing is an ordinary product, its location and facts go in the name and short text, and the For Sale / For Rent tag is the product badge.
- Header: the template's "For Rent / For Sale" mega menus are the store's own menu; the hover mega menu is not built. The header is solid black at 90% with no blur, on purpose (a blurred header breaks the fixed dialogs).
- Left out (no feature, or made-up text): the hero's location search box and the "Popular:" city links, the animated counters (1200+ properties, 4.9 rating), the script-font "More Than Just Properties" panel (needs a script font), the inquiry form and the WhatsApp / schedule-a-call buttons beside a listing (the store's chat button and the Contact page cover it), the gallery, the sign-in pop-up (StorePal's own).
- The footer is black and re-points colour variables, so the call bar (generated by `wire.js`) uses fixed colours: a gold band with white buttons.

## Needs an eye in the browser
Hero (a store's cover photo behind the black fades: a bright photo is darkened a lot), the black header over cream pages (it is solid, not transparent over the hero), the gold deeper tone on tags and prices, listing cards with a long name and location line, the dark features block with only one or two highlights, the contact bar when a store has no campaign (it does not show), the call bar, product page and cart (Furnilux/Menaura layout in gold).
