Template: `templete/others-templetes/General/viva/viva` (VIVA, general store, "Live Better").
Design (colours are exact, from the template's `tailwind.config.ts`): warm cream page `#faf8f4` with white rounded-3xl panels and a soft green shadow; deep forest green `#0d3b2e` (hover `#124a39`, deepest `#082a20` for the footer) for the top bar, buttons, the search button and the footer; sage `#d9e1b5` / pale `#eef2dc`; coral `#ef7b7b` discount tags; ink `#1c2a24`; **Fraunces** serif headings (exists, `--font-fraunces`) over **Inter** (exists). A cream header with a big pill search bar (green border, green button), a menu row with a green "All products" button, a soft sage hero card (serif headline, the picture fading in from the right, a green "sale" tag hanging from the top right when a badge is set), a white panel with a row of round category pictures, white "Top picks" panels with 5-across rounded product cards (coral tag, small round green cart button), three pastel promo tiles, a white feature strip, a dark green footer.
Built 2026-11-15 on the Novella kit's code (renamed `Viva*`, prefix `vv-`, tokens `--vv-*`), because Novella already has a cream header with a wide search bar and a menu row under it, and a dark footer; recoloured and restyled. Shared-file branches are copied from the `chicwove` ones like the other kits.

## Install
1. `node templete-ready-for-regantify/viva/wire.js` from the workspace root (theme, demo route, CSS block, enum + migration, `viva` branch next to every `chicwove` one in 29 shared files, THEME_ORDER entry last, `Trending Products` as the top-selling title). No new font. No `THEME_CATEGORIES` line (a general template shows under Others). Rerun-safe.
2. Server: `prisma generate` (stop the API first), `migrate deploy` locally and on production through the tunnel, `tsc --noEmit`.
3. `tsc --noEmit` in storefront, `tsc -b` in client.
4. Browser check on `shopnet` only: home, list, product, cart, wishlist, checkout, account, Bangla switch, `/template-demo/viva`, phone width, footer.
5. CLAUDE.md line, commit as Antor, push only when the owner says.

## Decisions and notes
- Plan: last in `THEME_ORDER`. Category: none (Others).
- Home order: HERO, CATEGORY_SHORTCUTS (round pictures), TOP_SELLING (white "Top picks" panel), FLASH_SALE, CAMPAIGNS (the three pastel tiles from a store's own campaigns), CATEGORY_SECTIONS, HIGHLIGHTS (white strip), REVIEWS, MAGAZINE, FAQ, SOCIAL_FEED.
- Header: the green top bar is StorePal's Site Banner (no banner text, no bar). The template's "All Categories" dropdown is a green "All products" button (the store's own categories are in the menu and the round category row). The template's mobile search under the logo is the search icon + shared search panel. The "Brands / Inspiration" links, which go nowhere in the template, are not drawn (the store's own menu shows).
- Card: the template puts the price and the cart button on one row; here the round cart button sits under the price, on the right (the shared card layout has a price row of its own).
- Left out (no feature, or made-up text): the newsletter block (no email feature), the template's testimonials (a store's own reviews show), the sample "Summer Sale 40%" tag (only drawn if a store sets a badge), the two small picture thumbnails on the hero, the demo toasts.
- The footer is dark green and re-points colour variables, so the call bar (generated by `wire.js`) uses fixed colours: a green band with white buttons.
