Design (colours exact, from the template's `tailwind.config.ts`): cream page `#FFFBF7`, orange `#F26A0F` (deeper `#D95A06`, pale `#FFF1E6`) for buttons and highlights, forest green `#1F5C34` for the green offer tile and the Site Banner bar, near-black ink `#16130F`, a cream light footer, **Plus Jakarta Sans** everywhere (new, added by `fonts-patch.js`) with extra-bold headings; a peach gradient hero card (photo fading in from the right, an orange "Order Now" and a white "Explore Menu" button, slide arrows are StorePal's dots), four soft feature cards, cuisine cards tinted green, lime, peach, pink and lilac with round photos, dish cards tinted soft peach with a round photo (white ring), name, rating and a small square orange add button, a green and a peach offer tile with a round dish photo spilling out of the corner.
Built 2026-11-15 on the Shopease kit's code (renamed `Tastehouse*`, prefix `th-`, tokens `--th-*`); recoloured and restyled. Shared-file branches are copied from the `chicwove` ones like the other kits.

## Install
1. `node templete-ready-for-regantify/tastehouse/wire.js` from the workspace root (theme, demo route, CSS block, Plus Jakarta Sans in `lib/fonts.ts`, enum + migration, `tastehouse` branch next to every `chicwove` one in 29 shared files, THEME_ORDER entry last, Templates category **Grocery & Food**). Rerun-safe.
2. Server: `prisma generate` (stop the API first), `migrate deploy` locally and on production through the tunnel, `tsc --noEmit`.
3. `tsc --noEmit` in storefront, `tsc -b` in client.
4. Browser check on `shopnet` only: home, list, product, cart, wishlist, checkout, account, Bangla switch, `/template-demo/tastehouse`, phone width, footer.
5. CLAUDE.md line, commit as Antor, push only when the owner says.

## Decisions and notes
- Plan: last in `THEME_ORDER`. Category: Grocery & Food.
- Home order: HERO, HIGHLIGHTS (soft cards), CATEGORY_SHORTCUTS (cuisine cards), TOP_SELLING (5 across), CAMPAIGNS (the green and peach tiles from a store's own campaigns, up to two), FLASH_SALE, CATEGORY_SECTIONS, REVIEWS, MAGAZINE, FAQ, SOCIAL_FEED.
- **The template's left sidebar is not built.** A skin cannot add a sidebar to the shared page layout. Its links (Home, Menu, Categories, Offers, My Orders, Help) are the header menu and the store's own pages; its "Get 20% OFF" box is a campaign tile.
- Header: a cream bar with no border and no blur; the template's search pill is the shared search icon and panel. The forest green bar above it is StorePal's Site Banner (no banner text, no bar).
- Left out (no feature, or made-up text): the sidebar, the floating dish card and the slide arrows on the hero, the "Trusted by" logo marquee, the newsletter strip, the promo codes (TASTY30) and the cart panels, the cards' cuisine filter.
- The footer is light cream, so the call bar (generated by `wire.js`) is an orange band with white buttons.
