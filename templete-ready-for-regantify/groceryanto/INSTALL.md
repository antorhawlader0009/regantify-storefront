# Groceryanto: install into Regantify (not installed yet, kit only)

Template: `templete/others-templetes/Grocery and Food/Grocery/groceryanto/groceryanto` (GROCERYANTO, grocery, a WooCommerce-style shop).
Design (colours exact, from the template's `tailwind.config.ts`): plain white, vivid green `#1cb63b` (deeper `#008000`) for buttons and links, ink `#111`, body text `#444`, muted `#767676`, thin `#ebebeb` lines, a light grey footer `#f7f8f9`, white cards with a pale green border `#b3dcbc` (green on hover) and centred names and prices, **Jost** headings over **Instrument Sans** with a little letter spacing (both new, added by `fonts-patch.js`). A white header (a 32px text logo, a wide 550px search bar with a black "Search" button, heart, account and a cart button with the total), under it a thin-lined row with a green "All Categories" button and the menu links; one wide hero banner inside the container with a soft white fade and the words on the left; a plain row of round category photos with a pale green ring; 5-across cards with a full-width green "Add to cart" button; a wide pale green promo banner; a benefits row with big green line icons and green Jost titles.
Built 2026-11-15 on the Viva kit's code (renamed `Groceryanto*`, prefix `ga-`, tokens `--ga-*`) because its header already has the search bar plus the green button and menu row; recoloured and restyled, and its dark footer turned light. Shared-file branches are copied from the `chicwove` ones like the other kits.

## Install
1. `node templete-ready-for-regantify/groceryanto/wire.js` from the workspace root (theme, demo route, CSS block, Instrument Sans and Jost in `lib/fonts.ts`, enum + migration, `groceryanto` branch next to every `chicwove` one in 29 shared files, THEME_ORDER entry last, Templates category **Grocery & Food**, `Trending Products` as the top-selling title). Rerun-safe.
2. Server: `prisma generate` (stop the API first), `migrate deploy` locally and on production through the tunnel, `tsc --noEmit`.
3. `tsc --noEmit` in storefront, `tsc -b` in client.
4. Browser check on `shopnet` only: home, list, product, cart, wishlist, checkout, account, Bangla switch, `/template-demo/groceryanto`, phone width, footer.
5. CLAUDE.md line, commit as Antor, push only when the owner says.

## Decisions and notes
- Plan: last in `THEME_ORDER`. Category: Grocery & Food.
- **The template's hero and promo banner are pictures with the words baked in.** A store has no such picture, so the hero is the store's cover photo with a white fade and the words written as text (the Site Banner and hero settings), and the promo banner is a pale green panel with the store's own campaign.
- Home order: HERO, CATEGORY_SHORTCUTS (the template has none; a plain round-photo row is added so a store's categories are reachable), TOP_SELLING, CAMPAIGNS, FLASH_SALE, CATEGORY_SECTIONS, HIGHLIGHTS (the benefits row), REVIEWS, MAGAZINE, FAQ, SOCIAL_FEED.
- **The template's hover pop-up on a card (Add to cart, Wishlist, Compare) is not built:** it does not work on a phone, so the green Add to cart button is always shown; the wishlist heart sits on the photo. Compare was removed from the platform.
- Header: the All Categories button opens the products page (a dropdown with sub-menus is not built); the template's slide-in phone menu is the shared menu.
- Left out (no feature, or made-up text): the "Join Risk Free / 14 days refund" and "Free delivery over $29" sample texts (a store writes its own Highlights), the footer's lorem-ipsum about text, the compare bar, the quick-view pop-up.
- The footer is light grey, so the call bar (generated by `wire.js`) is a green band above it with white buttons.

## Needs an eye in the browser
Hero (a store's cover photo with the white fade: on a dark photo the words stay readable but the fade looks milky; on a phone the box is 300px tall), the round category row for a store with no category pictures (first letter shown), square card photos for wide or tall pictures, the black Search button text (the shared search panel opens on a click), the green band call bar on a light footer, product page and cart (Furnilux/Menaura layout in green).
