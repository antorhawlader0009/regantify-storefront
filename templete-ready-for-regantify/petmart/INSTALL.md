# Petmart: install into Regantify (not installed yet, kit only)

Template: `templete/others-templetes/Kids & Pets/petmart/petmart` (PETMART, pet store, "For a Brighter, Furrier Tomorrow").
Design (colours exact, from the template's `tailwind.config.ts`): a white page, ember orange `#e8622a` (deeper `#cf5120`) for the main buttons, the search button and tags, pine green `#1d4a3c` (soft `#e4efe9`) for the top bar, the footer, the round cart buttons and the features strip, a peach hero card `#fff3ea` / `#fdddc9`, sky `#d6e8f6`, slate-grey thin lines, **Poppins** everywhere (exists, `--font-poppins`). A white header (a bold pine "PetMart" wordmark, a slate search pill with an ember button, the menu row under it, heart, account and cart), a peach hero card (an uppercase spaced "LOVE . CARE . SHOP" line, a huge extra-bold pine title, an ember pill "Shop Now", the pet photo on the right under a peach fade), a slate panel with a row of tinted round category photos, 5-across white cards (4:3.6 photo, semibold name, stars, a big price, a round pine cart button that turns ember on hover), peach and sky offer tiles with a big ember title, a pine-soft features strip, a dark pine footer.
Built 2026-11-15 on the Viva kit's code (renamed `Petmart*`, prefix `pk-`, tokens `--pk-*`; `pm-` is taken by PlayMate and `pt-` would clash with Tailwind's padding) because it already has a hero card, a pill search, a menu row and a dark green footer; recoloured and restyled. Shared-file branches are copied from the `chicwove` ones like the other kits.

## Install
1. `node templete-ready-for-regantify/petmart/wire.js` from the workspace root (theme, demo route, CSS block, enum + migration, `petmart` branch next to every `chicwove` one in 29 shared files, THEME_ORDER entry last, Templates category **Kids & Pets**, `Top Selling` as the top-selling title). No new font. Rerun-safe.
2. Server: `prisma generate` (stop the API first), `migrate deploy` locally and on production through the tunnel, `tsc --noEmit`.
3. `tsc --noEmit` in storefront, `tsc -b` in client.
4. Browser check on `shopnet` only: home, list, product, cart, wishlist, checkout, account, Bangla switch, `/template-demo/petmart`, phone width, footer.
5. CLAUDE.md line, commit as Antor, push only when the owner says.

## What was verified (2026-11-15, repos untouched)
`wire.js` ran on a scratch copy of the storefront/server/client files (junction to node_modules); `tsc --noEmit` there was clean on the first run. Client and server were not type-checked (theme-name lists only), nothing was looked at in a browser. Repos stayed at 0 changes.

## Decisions and notes
- Plan: last in `THEME_ORDER`. Category: Kids & Pets.
- Home order: HERO, CATEGORY_SHORTCUTS (the slate panel of round photos), TOP_SELLING, FLASH_SALE, CATEGORY_SECTIONS, CAMPAIGNS (the peach and sky tiles from a store's own campaigns, up to three), HIGHLIGHTS (the pine-soft strip), REVIEWS, MAGAZINE, FAQ, SOCIAL_FEED.
- Header: the template puts the menu under the search bar in the middle column; here the menu is a row under the whole top row (the shared header layout). The pine top bar is StorePal's Site Banner (no banner text, no bar), so its "Free shipping / PAW10" lines show only if the store writes them. The template's pill search is the shared search icon and panel.
- **The template's category circles hold emoji; here they hold the store's own category photos** (the first letter on a tinted circle when a store has no picture). No emoji anywhere.
- Left out (no feature, or made-up text): the hero's three small icons (Better Nutrition, Happier Pets, Trusted Brands), the handwritten "Pets Make Life Brighter" paw badge (needs the Caveat font; the hero's sale tag is only drawn if a store sets a badge), the "Seasonal Collection" row (a store's collections cover it), the newsletter, the back-to-top button, the PAW10 code.
- The footer is dark pine green and re-points colour variables, so the call bar (generated by `wire.js`) uses fixed colours: an ember orange band with white buttons.
