Template: `templete/others-templetes/Beauty and Health/glowerive/glowerive` (GLOWERIVE, natural skincare).
Design: teal `#1c6f65` header and full-height hero (photo fading into teal), pale mist page `#f4f9f8`, deep teal `#0f4a42` top strip and footer, dark text `#0b3a34`, Cormorant Garamond headings (`--font-cormorant`) over Inter, pill buttons, round category photos with a pale ring (`#e6f0ee`), white product cards with a thin pale ring, a pale feature panel that overlaps the hero, a teal gradient offer band.
Built 2026-11-15 on the Drivecraft kit's code (renamed `Glowerive*`, prefix `gw-`, tokens `--gw-*`), recoloured and restyled. Shared-file branches are copied from the `chicwove` ones like the other kits.

## Install
1. `node templete-ready-for-regantify/glowerive/wire.js` from the workspace root (theme, demo route, CSS block, enum + migration, `glowerive` branch next to every `chicwove` one in 29 shared files, THEME_ORDER entry last, Templates category `Beauty & Health`). No new font needed. Rerun-safe.
2. Server: `prisma generate` (stop the API first), `migrate deploy` locally and on production through the tunnel, `tsc --noEmit`.
3. `tsc --noEmit` in storefront, `tsc -b` in client.
4. Browser check on `shopnet` only: home, list, product, cart, wishlist, checkout, account, Bangla switch, `/template-demo/glowerive`, phone width, footer.
5. CLAUDE.md line, commit as Antor, push only when the owner says.

## Decisions and notes
- Plan: last in `THEME_ORDER`. Category on the Templates page: `Beauty & Health`.
- Home order: HERO, HIGHLIGHTS (the pale panel overlaps the hero with `-mt-14`, so keep it second), CATEGORY_SHORTCUTS, TOP_SELLING (called "Featured Products", 4 across), FLASH_SALE, CAMPAIGNS (the teal offer band), CATEGORY_SECTIONS, REVIEWS, MAGAZINE, FAQ, SOCIAL_FEED.
- Header and top bar are teal with white text, the footer deep teal; the footer re-points colour variables (accent becomes pale teal `#9fc9c2`), so the call bar (`FooterCallBar` look `glowerive`, generated by `wire.js`) uses fixed colours: a teal band with white pill buttons.
- Left out (no feature, or made-up text): "Watch Video" button, the "Join Now / 20% off" sign-up form (the offer band shows the store's own campaigns), the template's sample promises (the store's Highlights show), the sample "Free shipping over $50" strip (the store's Site Banner shows), the service strip with its copyright line, the leaf logo mark (drawn only when the store has no logo picture).
- Product cards: white, rounded, 5:4.4 photo on pale teal, serif-free name, dark price, pill Add to Cart (teal, deepens on hover); Buy Now line button under it (always visible rule).
