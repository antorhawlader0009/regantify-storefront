## Install
1. `node templete-ready-for-regantify/gadgetstore/wire.js` from the workspace root (theme, demo route, CSS block, enum + migration, `gadgetstore` branch next to every `chicwove` one in 29 shared files, THEME_ORDER entry last). No new font. Category: adds `GADGETSTORE: ['Technology & Gadgets']` in `Themes.tsx` (same tab as TechZone). Rerun-safe.
2. Server: `prisma generate` (stop the API first), `migrate deploy` locally and on production through the tunnel, `tsc --noEmit`.
3. `tsc --noEmit` in storefront, `tsc -b` in client.
4. Browser check on `shopnet` only: home, list, product, cart, wishlist, checkout, account, Bangla switch, `/template-demo/gadgetstore`, phone width, footer.
5. CLAUDE.md line, commit as Antor, push only when the owner says.

## Decisions and notes
- Plan: last in `THEME_ORDER`. Category: Technology & Gadgets.
- Home order: HERO, HIGHLIGHTS (the benefits row), CATEGORY_SHORTCUTS (round pictures), TOP_SELLING, CAMPAIGNS (the black and light promo tiles from a store's own campaigns), FLASH_SALE, CATEGORY_SECTIONS, REVIEWS, MAGAZINE, FAQ, SOCIAL_FEED.
- **Lime needs dark text, and lime is not used as text on white.** Every lime-filled button carries dark text; `--color-accent` is black (like Urbanx and Sporteq), so shared buttons (checkout, cart) are black with white text and only the hero, promo, call-bar and tag fills are lime.
- Header: the template's inline search box with a live results drop-down is the shared search panel (an icon). The "Shop / Categories" hover drop-downs are not built (the store's own menu shows). The black top bar is StorePal's Site Banner (no banner text, no bar).
- Cards: the template shows the black Add to Cart bar over the picture on hover (always on a phone); here it sits under the card. The small blue brand word (e.g. ANKER) on a picture is not shown.
- Left out (no feature, or made-up text): the price slider and filters on the product list (StorePal's own list has its own), the Blog and About Us pages, the cart and wishlist pop-ups (StorePal's own), the sample "10% off your first order" top bar lines, the newsletter band.
- The footer is white, so the call bar (generated by `wire.js`) is a black band with lime buttons.

## Needs an eye in the browser
Hero (a store's cover photo with blended multiply: a dark or busy photo does not blend like a product on white, it may need a plain background), round grey category pictures for a store with no pictures (first letter shown), the black Add to Cart bars on every card (heavier than the template's hover bars), the lime tags next to black text, five-across cards at 1280 px, the call bar, product page and cart (Furnilux/Menaura layout, black accent).
