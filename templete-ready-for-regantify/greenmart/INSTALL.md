# Greenmart: install into Regantify (not installed yet, kit only)

Template: `templete/others-templetes/Grocery and Food/Grocery/greenmart/greenmart` (GREENMART, grocery, "Fresh Groceries, Delivered").
Design (colours exact, from the template's `tailwind.config.ts`): white page, leaf green `#1c6b22` (hover `#165619`, mid `#2f8a35`) for buttons, tags and links, pale green `#f1f6e8` / `#e4edd3` panels, cream `#f8f5ef`, ink `#1d2a1f`, a deep green footer `#10301a`, **Inter** everywhere (exists) with extra-bold headings and a soft green shadow. A white header (logo left, Search / Favorites / Account / cart right, the menu in a row under it between thin lines), a full-width pale green hero (the photo on the right fading left, a dark green eyebrow pill, a huge title with a green second line, a green and an outline button), a white category panel floating over the hero's lower edge (title row, six round photos), a pale green deal / offer panel with the picture masked in on the right, 4-across white cards (4:3 pale green photo, name, big price, stars, a square green cart button), a plain "Why Choose" row with round soft icons and thin dividers.
Built 2026-11-15 on the Viva kit's code (renamed `Greenmart*`, prefix `gm-`, tokens `--gm-*`) because it already has a dark green footer and a header with a menu row; recoloured and restyled. Shared-file branches are copied from the `chicwove` ones like the other kits.

## Install
1. `node templete-ready-for-regantify/greenmart/wire.js` from the workspace root (theme, demo route, CSS block, enum + migration, `greenmart` branch next to every `chicwove` one in 29 shared files, THEME_ORDER entry last, Templates category **Grocery & Food**, `Trending Products` as the top-selling title). No new font. Rerun-safe.
2. Server: `prisma generate` (stop the API first), `migrate deploy` locally and on production through the tunnel, `tsc --noEmit`.
3. `tsc --noEmit` in storefront, `tsc -b` in client.
4. Browser check on `shopnet` only: home, list, product, cart, wishlist, checkout, account, Bangla switch, `/template-demo/greenmart`, phone width, footer.
5. CLAUDE.md line, commit as Antor, push only when the owner says.

## What was verified (2026-11-15, repos untouched)
`wire.js` ran on a scratch copy of the storefront/server/client files (junction to node_modules); `tsc --noEmit` there was clean on the first run. Client and server were not type-checked (theme-name lists only), nothing was looked at in a browser. Repos stayed at 0 changes.

## Decisions and notes
- Plan: last in `THEME_ORDER`. Category: Grocery & Food.
- Home order: HERO, CATEGORY_SHORTCUTS (floating panel), FLASH_SALE (the template's "Fresh Deals" panel with three cards is StorePal's flash sale section), CAMPAIGNS (pale green offer panels from a store's own campaigns, up to two), TOP_SELLING, CATEGORY_SECTIONS, HIGHLIGHTS ("Why Choose" row), REVIEWS, MAGAZINE, FAQ, SOCIAL_FEED.
- Header: the big search pill of the Viva kit is gone; the search is a plain icon (a "Search" word is not drawn) that opens the shared search panel. The green top bar is StorePal's Site Banner (no banner text, no bar). The scroll-spy menu highlight (the green underline on the section in view) is not built; the shared menu links show as plain text.
- Left out (no feature, or made-up text): the hero's three perk icons ("Fresh & Quality", "Fast Delivery", "Secure & Safe"), the newsletter strip (no email feature), the delivery banner text ("Free delivery over $49", a store sets its own), the footer's italic serif tagline, the search modal's "popular searches", the sample "Just landed" kicker.
- The footer is deep green and re-points colour variables, so the call bar (generated by `wire.js`) uses fixed colours: a leaf green band with white buttons.

## Needs an eye in the browser
Hero (a store's cover photo across 60% of the width on a computer, on top on a phone; with a dark photo the pale left fade may look milky), the floating category panel for a store with few categories or no pictures (first letter shown), the 4:3 card photo for tall product pictures, the pale green offer panel with a campaign picture, the call bar colours, product page and cart (Furnilux/Menaura layout in green).
