## Install
1. `node templete-ready-for-regantify/quickbite/wire.js` from the workspace root (theme, demo route, CSS block, enum + migration, `quickbite` branch next to every `chicwove` one in 29 shared files, THEME_ORDER entry last, Templates category **Grocery & Food**, `Top Selling` as the top-selling title). No new font. Rerun-safe.
2. Server: `prisma generate` (stop the API first), `migrate deploy` locally and on production through the tunnel, `tsc --noEmit`.
3. `tsc --noEmit` in storefront, `tsc -b` in client.
4. Browser check on `shopnet` only: home, list, product, cart, wishlist, checkout, account, Bangla switch, `/template-demo/quickbite`, phone width, footer.
5. CLAUDE.md line, commit as Antor, push only when the owner says.

## Decisions and notes
- Plan: last in `THEME_ORDER`. Category: Grocery & Food.
- Home order: HERO, CATEGORY_SHORTCUTS (round photos), TOP_SELLING (4-across dish cards), FLASH_SALE (the template's "Today's Deals" is StorePal's flash sale section), CATEGORY_SECTIONS, CAMPAIGNS (the big glass-card banner from a store's own campaign, up to two), HIGHLIGHTS (the glass strip), REVIEWS, MAGAZINE, FAQ, SOCIAL_FEED. The template's per-category rows (Burgers, Sushi, Desserts) are the shared category sections.
- **The page background is the gradient on the skin wrapper.** The header is a solid warm bar (about 90% opaque) with **no backdrop blur**, on purpose: a blurred header breaks the fixed menu and search dialogs. Glass is used on cards and panels only.
- Left out (no feature, or made-up text): the hero's "enter your delivery address" box and its "Fast Delivery / Fresh Food / Secure Payment" icons, the handwritten "Fresh & Tasty" note (needs the Caveat font), the two floating round photos on the hero, the restaurants block, the cards' delivery time ("25-35 min"; a store has no such field), the "Healthy Choices" sample text, the emoji fallbacks.
- The footer is dark brown and re-points colour variables, so the call bar (generated by `wire.js`) uses fixed colours: an amber band with white buttons.
