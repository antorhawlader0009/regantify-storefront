# Furnilux: install into Regantify (not installed yet, kit only)

Template: `templete/others-templetes/Furniture & Home/furnilux/furnilux` (FURNILUX, furniture store).
Design: white page, forest green `#3f6b1f` (deeper `#2f5016`, pale `#e9f0e1`) as the accent and button fill, sale red `#e11d2e` for sale tags, warm cream `#f7f5f0` hero card, grey panels `#f4f4f2`, near-black `#171717` footer, **Plus Jakarta Sans** everywhere (new, added by `fonts-patch.js`) with extra-bold headings, white header with uppercase 11px links, a rounded cream hero card with the picture and a cream fade from the left, three big room cards plus three small ones, bordered rounded product cards with a 4:3 photo, a grey brand strip.
Built 2026-11-15 on the Menaura kit's code (renamed `Furnilux*`, prefix `fu-`, tokens `--fu-*`), recoloured and restyled. Shared-file branches are copied from the `chicwove` ones like the other kits.

## Install
1. `node templete-ready-for-regantify/furnilux/wire.js` from the workspace root (theme, demo route, CSS block, Plus Jakarta Sans in `lib/fonts.ts`, enum + migration, `furnilux` branch next to every `chicwove` one in 29 shared files, THEME_ORDER entry last, Templates category `Furniture & Home`). Rerun-safe.
2. Server: `prisma generate` (stop the API first), `migrate deploy` locally and on production through the tunnel, `tsc --noEmit`.
3. `tsc --noEmit` in storefront, `tsc -b` in client.
4. Browser check on `shopnet` only: home, list, product, cart, wishlist, checkout, account, Bangla switch, `/template-demo/furnilux`, phone width, footer.
5. CLAUDE.md line, commit as Antor, push only when the owner says.

## Decisions and notes
- Plan: last in `THEME_ORDER`. Category on the Templates page: `Furniture & Home`.
- Home order: HERO, HIGHLIGHTS, CATEGORY_SHORTCUTS (the room cards: the first three big, the next three small; a store with fewer categories shows fewer), TOP_SELLING (called "Featured Products"), FLASH_SALE, CAMPAIGNS (green offer band), CATEGORY_SECTIONS, REVIEWS, MAGAZINE, FAQ, SOCIAL_FEED. The template's brand strip is StorePal's Brands row (a grey panel); its blog is the Magazine section.
- Header: the shared sticky header and category strip in white; the template's dropdown menus per room are not drawn (a store's own menu and categories show). The "Sale" pill is not drawn (a store may put a Sale link in its menu).
- Left out (no feature, or made-up text): the product card's compare and quick-view buttons (no compare feature, Add to Cart is always shown), colour dots on cards (not in the shared card), the footer newsletter box and the template's footer links (the store's own columns show), the "Stay updated" strip, the sample hero texts (demo only).
- The footer is near-black and re-points colour variables, so the call bar (`FooterCallBar` look `furnilux`, generated by `wire.js`) uses fixed colours: a green band with white buttons.

## Needs an eye in the browser
Hero card (the picture covers the card with a cream fade; a store's cover photo may crop differently), room cards for stores with few or no category pictures, product card sizes in 4 columns, extra-bold headings with Bangla text (Plus Jakarta has no Bangla letters, the Bangla font takes over), product page and cart (Menaura/Chicwove layout in green), the white header at 1024 to 1280 px.
