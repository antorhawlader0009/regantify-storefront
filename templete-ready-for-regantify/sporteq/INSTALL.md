# Sporteq: install into Regantify (installed 2026-11-17, browser-checked on shopnet)

Template: `templete/others-templetes/Sports/sporteq/sporteq` (SPORTEQ, sports equipment, "Equip Your Best").
Design (colours exact, from the template's `tailwind.config.ts`): a white page, a charcoal header `#1b1b1b` (top bar `#1a1a1a`), a black full-picture hero, lime green `#7dc400` (deeper `#69a600`, light `#9be21a`) for the main buttons **with dark text**, tags and the active tones, ink `#0e0e0e`, light grey `#f6f6f6` product cards with a small radius and a white photo, uppercase spaced button labels, a clean white footer with a thin line, **DM Sans** everywhere (exists) and an **Orbitron** wordmark (new, added by `fonts-patch.js`). A charcoal header (the Orbitron "SPORT/EQ" wordmark, uppercase menu with a lime "Deals", search, account, wishlist and a cart with a lime count), a full-picture hero (a small spaced lime eyebrow, a huge extra-bold white capitals title with a lime second line, a lime "Shop Now" and a white-outline "Explore Collection"), a light benefits strip, a centred "Shop by Category" title over a row of eight round pictures with a lime ring on hover, six-across grey cards, two promo banners (a black one with a lime button, a light grey one), a white footer.
Built 2026-11-15 on the Urbanx kit's code (renamed `Sporteq*`, prefix `eq-`, tokens `--eq-*`), because Urbanx already had a dark header, a full-picture dark hero, a lime accent with dark text, grey cards and the two promo banners; the graffiti wordmark became Orbitron, its dark footer became white and its grayscale category tiles became round pictures. Shared-file branches are copied from the `chicwove` ones like the other kits.

## Install
1. `node templete-ready-for-regantify/sporteq/wire.js` from the workspace root (theme, demo route, CSS block, Orbitron in `lib/fonts.ts`, enum + migration, `sporteq` branch next to every `chicwove` one in 29 shared files, THEME_ORDER entry last). No `THEME_CATEGORIES` line: Templates has no "Sports" tab, so it shows under Others (add the tab to `CATEGORIES` in `Themes.tsx` first if the owner wants one). Rerun-safe.
2. Server: `prisma generate` (stop the API first), `migrate deploy` locally and on production through the tunnel, `tsc --noEmit`.
3. `tsc --noEmit` in storefront, `tsc -b` in client.
4. Browser check on `shopnet` only: home, list, product, cart, wishlist, checkout, account, Bangla switch, `/template-demo/sporteq`, phone width, footer.
5. CLAUDE.md line, commit as Antor, push only when the owner says.

## What was verified (2026-11-15, repos untouched)
`wire.js` ran on a scratch copy of the storefront/server/client files (junction to node_modules); `tsc --noEmit` there was clean on the first run. Client and server were not type-checked (theme-name lists only), nothing was looked at in a browser. Repos stayed at 0 changes.

## Decisions and notes
- Plan: last in `THEME_ORDER`. Category: none (Others).
- Home order: HERO, HIGHLIGHTS (the benefits strip), CATEGORY_SHORTCUTS (round pictures), TOP_SELLING, CAMPAIGNS (the black and light grey promo banners from a store's own campaigns), FLASH_SALE, CATEGORY_SECTIONS, REVIEWS, MAGAZINE, FAQ, SOCIAL_FEED. The template's drag-to-scroll Best Sellers carousel is a normal grid here (2, 3, 5 and 6 across).
- **Lime needs dark text.** Buttons in the kit that are filled lime carry `#0e0e0e`; the shared "accent fill" buttons stay black with white text (`--color-accent` is `#0e0e0e`, like Urbanx), so Add to Cart / checkout buttons are black and only the hero, promo and call-bar buttons are lime.
- The Orbitron wordmark has "SPORT" and "EQ" in two colours in the template; a store's own name is one colour here (white on the charcoal header, black in the footer), unless a store uploads a logo picture.
- Left out (no feature, or made-up text): the SPORT10 code and the sample top bar lines (a store writes its own Site Banner), the hover "Add to Cart" bar over a card photo (a full-width black button is always shown; hover bars do not work on a phone), the Shop and Categories hover drop-downs, the blog and "About Us" links, the sign-in drop-down (StorePal's own).
- The footer is white, so the call bar (generated by `wire.js`) is a light grey band with dark text and a lime button.
