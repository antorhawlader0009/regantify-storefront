Template: `templete/others-templetes/Properties/evernest-homes/evernest-homes` (EVERNEST, eco-friendly homes, "Find Eco-Friendly Homes Easily").
Design (colours exact, from the template's `tailwind.config.ts`): a cream page `#f7f7f0` (darker `#eef0e1`), forest green `#46682d` (hover `#36501f`, deepest `#24361a` for text and the footer) for pill buttons, links and tags, soft lime `#a9d27d` highlights, pale forest `#e6ecda`, white rounded-2xl cards with a soft green shadow, **Playfair Display** serif headings (exists, `--font-playfair`) over **Inter** (exists). A cream header (a green leaf-circle logo with a serif name, forest menu links, round plain icon buttons, a green pill-shaped profile button), a full-width sunlit hero photo under a light black fade on the left (a cream pill eyebrow, a serif title with a lime second line, a forest pill button with a white outline), a soft "welcome" panel (a white-bordered photo and serif words with a forest button), four image category cards with an overlapping info panel, 3-across listing cards (4:3 photo, a rounded status tag, name, location line, bold price, a forest "View" pill), a dark forest trust block with glass cards, a deep green footer with a lime call band.
Built 2026-11-15 on the Estatepro kit's code (renamed `Evernest*`, prefix `ev-`, tokens `--ev-*`), because the two templates share the same layout (hero, category cards, why-choose block, listing cards, contact bar); gold became forest green and its black header cream. Shared-file branches are copied from the `chicwove` ones like the other kits.

## Install
1. `node templete-ready-for-regantify/evernest/wire.js` from the workspace root (theme, demo route, CSS block, enum + migration, `evernest` branch next to every `chicwove` one in 29 shared files, THEME_ORDER entry last). No new font. No `THEME_CATEGORIES` line: Templates has no "Properties" tab, so it shows under Others. Rerun-safe.
2. Server: `prisma generate` (stop the API first), `migrate deploy` locally and on production through the tunnel, `tsc --noEmit`.
3. `tsc --noEmit` in storefront, `tsc -b` in client.
4. Browser check on `shopnet` only: home, list, product, cart, wishlist, checkout, account, Bangla switch, `/template-demo/evernest`, phone width, footer.
5. CLAUDE.md line, commit as Antor, push only when the owner says.

## Decisions and notes
- Plan: last in `THEME_ORDER`. Category: none (Others).
- Home order: HERO, CAMPAIGNS (the soft "welcome" panel from a store's own campaign, up to two), CATEGORY_SHORTCUTS (four image cards), TOP_SELLING (listing cards), FLASH_SALE, CATEGORY_SECTIONS, HIGHLIGHTS (the dark block), REVIEWS, MAGAZINE, FAQ, SOCIAL_FEED.
- The template is a property catalogue (beds, baths, area, For Sale / For Rent, an Eco-Friendly tag, a search bar with location, type and price lists, a quick-view pop-up). StorePal has none of those fields: a listing is an ordinary product, its location and facts go in the name and short text, and the For Sale / For Rent tag is the product badge.
- The template's whole page sits inside a rounded card on a sage gradient frame; that frame is not built (it needs a wrapper around the shared page). The header is cream at 95% with no blur, on purpose (a blurred header breaks the fixed dialogs).
- Left out (no feature, or made-up text): the hero's "Popular:" city links, the script-font "More Than Just Properties" panel (needs a script font), the search bar under the hero (the shared search is in the header), the animated counters, the "Best Deals" round badge on the welcome picture, the property quick-view pop-up, the hover mega menu for Buy and Rent, the inquiry form.
- The footer is deep green, so the call bar (generated by `wire.js`) uses fixed colours: a lime band with white buttons.

## Needs an eye in the browser
Hero (a store's cover photo under a light fade: a pale photo makes the white title hard to read), the cream header over the photo (it is solid, not transparent), the welcome panel when a store has no campaign (it does not show), the lime tone on the dark trust block with one or two highlights, long names and location lines on cards, the call bar, product page and cart (Furnilux/Menaura layout in forest green).
